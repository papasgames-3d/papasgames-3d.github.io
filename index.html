About Papa's Games Series

Papa's Games is an acclaimed series of restaurant simulation games developed by Flipline Studios. Each game in the series puts you in the role of a restaurant employee who must take orders, prepare food, and serve customers to earn tips and upgrade your restaurant.

The Papa's Games Universe

The Papa's Games series takes place in a colorful world filled with unique characters and locations. The protagonist of most games is Papa Louie, a chef and entrepreneur who owns multiple restaurants throughout the fictional Tastyville and surrounding areas.

Popular Papa's Games Titles
  • Papa's Freezeria - Make delicious ice cream treats
  • Papa's Burgeria - Grill and stack perfect burgers
  • Papa's Pizzeria - Create custom pizzas with various toppings
  • Papa's Wingeria - Cook and sauce chicken wings
  • Papa's Sushiria - Roll sushi and brew tea
Key Features of Papa's Games
  1. Multiple game stations for order taking, food preparation, and serving
  2. Custom character creation in many titles
  3. Progressive difficulty with increasingly complex orders
  4. Shop upgrades to improve efficiency and customer satisfaction
  5. Special holiday celebrations with themed ingredients and recipes
